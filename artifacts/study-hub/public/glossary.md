# Study Hub Glossary

## Subject

A course or area of study that groups lectures, attachments, flashcards, exams, and progress information.

## Lecture

A unit of learning inside a subject. Lectures may be theoretical or practical and can contain links, attachments, questions, and flashcards.

## Exam

An assessment associated with a subject. Study Hub can store exam dates, questions, attempts, and performance results.

## Exam schedule

A plan that connects exams or subjects to specific dates and times.

## Study schedule

A plan for studying selected subjects over a date range, optionally using repeated days and start or end times.

## Checklist

A collection of study tasks that can be completed, skipped, grouped, or reviewed.

## Flashcard

A prompt-and-answer study item created from lecture or subject material.

## Attachment

A file, link, or supporting resource associated with a subject or lecture.

## Progress

A summary calculated from completed lectures, exam results, and other study activity.

## Archive

A holding area for removed records that may be restored or permanently deleted.
