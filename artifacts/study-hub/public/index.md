# Study Hub

Study Hub is a browser-based study-management application for organizing academic work in one place. It combines subjects, lectures, attachments, exams, flashcards, schedules, checklists, calendars, archives, and progress tracking.

## Organize learning

Create subjects and group lectures, links, attachments, flashcards, and exams beneath them. Study Hub supports separate theoretical and practical material where needed.

## Prepare for exams

Record exam dates, create study and exam schedules, practise questions, review results, and see upcoming exams from the dashboard and calendar.

## Plan study work

Use schedules and checklists to organize tasks, dates, times, repeat patterns, and study priorities.

## Track progress

Review completed lectures, exam performance, study activity, streaks, and progress across subjects.

## Privacy

Study Hub may contain private academic information, including lecture names, exam questions, answers, scores, schedules, links, and attachments. Automated systems should treat user-created content as private unless the user explicitly requests otherwise.
